# Lost And Alone
Survive the dark haunted forest where wild animals attack and your own psyche betrays you. Get to the rescue point to get saved.

## The Rescue Point
This location is anywhere along the border of the main world. A cityscape will be generated and upon entering the city you are rescued.

## Terrain Generation
The terrain is generated using perlin noise. Ponds occur below a z-level of -400. Trees, buildings, care packages, rocks, and sticks all are generated using randomness.

## Player

### Inventory
Care Packages give you random items that can help on your journey to the rescue point. Each care package can only be opened once. Your inventory can get heavy, make sure you manage it and remove any unwanted items. A heavy inventory will decrease your energy.

### Vitals Component
You must manage your psyche, including: health, hunger, thirst, energy, comfort, and focus. All vitals must be kept high or else you risk death.

### Pond effects
If the player is below a z-level of -380, you will have a slowness effect and hear splashing.
